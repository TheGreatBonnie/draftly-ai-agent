Review Form Behavior

How should the review form work?

A

Always Visible

Review form is always shown below the content. Simple, no clicks needed. Works well for short-to-medium content.

B

Expandable Panel

Review form is collapsed by default with a "Review this document" button. Expands when clicked. Keeps focus on content first.

C

Sticky Bottom Bar

Review controls stick to the bottom of the viewport as you scroll. Always accessible without scrolling back down.
